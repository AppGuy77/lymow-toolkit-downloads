Lymow Toolkit v2.10.0

An update for everyone on v2.9. It keeps your sign-in, settings, maps, layout and history — just update.


- **Drive your mower with a game controller** (Xbox, PlayStation or MFi) paired to your phone, tablet or computer — from the Remote tab.
- **Picking up the controller starts the camera at fullscreen** on the Remote tab, so on a phone you don't switch between the two.
- **Set it up once per kind of device** — find it, choose up / down / left / right, assign the buttons; on-screen prompts can be answered from the pad too.
- **Landscape on a phone now matches portrait** — one column turned sideways, no columns running off the screen.


## Game controller

- **Drive from the Remote tab with a paired controller.** An Xbox, PlayStation or MFi controller connected to your
  phone, tablet or computer drives the mower just like the on-screen joystick — the left stick steers (how far you push
  it is how fast it goes), and it moves the mower only while the live camera picture is up, the same as the joystick.
- **Pick up the controller, the camera starts.** On the Remote tab, using the controller turns the camera on at
  fullscreen by itself; the Start button turns the camera (and the controls) on and off.
- **Set up controller** — once per kind of device (iPhone, Android or computer): find it, choose up / down / left /
  right, then assign the buttons for blade speed, raising and lowering the deck, drive speed up and down, the speed
  level, stop the blades, camera on/off, and Confirm / Cancel for on-screen menus. Most Xbox, PlayStation and MFi
  controllers work with no setup. A ✓ beside **Set up controller** shows once a kind of device is set up.
- **Answer prompts from the controller.** When a prompt like "Turn on the blades?" appears, the Confirm and Cancel
  buttons work it, so you never reach for the screen.
- **The buttons tell you what changed.** Blade speed, deck height, drive speed and the speed level each flash on screen
  as you change them.

## Phones

- **Landscape matches portrait.** The Overview and Remote pages stay one column when you turn the phone sideways, in the
  same order, instead of splitting into narrow columns that ran off the screen.
