Lymow Toolkit v2.12.0

Official app 3.0.10 parity, Do Not Disturb by the sun and the dew, and your zones' custom settings kept. It keeps your sign-in, settings, maps, layout and history — just update.


- **Cross-cut at your own angle** — the checkered pattern with a stripe angle you choose, in the cutting settings and in schedules.
- **Ignore weeds & saplings** — the official app's switch, in Settings → Mowing options.
- **Do Not Disturb by the sun and the dew** — mowing hours set every day from sunrise, sunset and the dew forecast, written to the mower so the times show in the official app (the forecast only after you agree to share your area).
- **Mowing should no longer reset your zones' custom settings.**


## New

- **Cross-cut at your own angle.** The checkered (cross-cut) pattern now works with a stripe angle you choose, not only with Optimized — in the cutting settings and in schedules — the same way the official app 3.0.10 does it.
- **Ignore weeds & saplings.** Settings → Mowing options has the official app's switch: with Smart obstacle avoidance on, the mower drives through weeds and thin saplings instead of steering around them.
- **Do Not Disturb hours, by the sun and the dew.** Settings → Recharge & Resume (Do Not Disturb) is the official app's Do Not Disturb window. Choose **Sun & dew** and the Toolkit sets the hours every day and writes them to the mower, so they hold with the Toolkit closed — the Start Time and End Time show in the official app, the same as if you had typed them there: start a set number of minutes after sunrise or when the weather forecast says the morning dew has dried, and stop at sunset or when the forecast says dew sets in. The forecast comes from Open-Meteo, only after you agree to share your mower's area (rounded to about 11 km, never the exact spot); Stop sharing ends it at any time.
- **The new Lymow notifications are recognized** (app 3.0.10): Do Not Disturb, RTK and overheating notices are named correctly.

## Fixed

- **Mowing should no longer reset your zones' custom settings.** A mow start, a one-time mow, a scheduled mow or a pattern change no longer overwrites the custom stripe angle, cut height or perimeter laps of your zones — including zones not in that mow. Only **Override zones** does that, on purpose. Settings already lost have to be entered once more.
- **The Home Assistant Remote card updates itself.** The card's frame now reloads after a Toolkit update, so an old page can no longer sit there refusing to drive ("a drive command names no mower"); a page that is out of date is told to reload.
- **Zone-settings saves show in the Event log** (Advanced Data → Diagnostics) with what was sent and the mower's answer.
- **More of the Toolkit in your language.**
