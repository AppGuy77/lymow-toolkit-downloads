Lymow Toolkit v2.11.0

Edit your map without Bluetooth, zone names that match the Lymow app, and smoother remote driving. It keeps your sign-in, settings, maps, layout and history — just update.


- **Edit your map without Bluetooth** — rename, delete, merge and split zones over the cloud, and draw new zones, no-go areas, channels and boundaries with the live camera and joystick.
- **Zone names match the Lymow app** — a zone you rename in the Toolkit shows the same name in the official app.
- **Smoother, safer remote driving** — lower latency, fewer dropped frames, a seamless WiFi→4G hand-off, and leaving remote control wakes the mower so it can never be left stranded.


## Map editing

- From the Remote tab you can rename, delete, merge and split zones over the cloud — no phone on Bluetooth and no standing next to the mower.
- An **Edit Map** button on the camera (and in Remote Options) lets you create a new zone, no-go area or channel and redraw a boundary by driving the mower with the live picture and the joystick. New areas save themselves when they close; a channel saves when it reaches the next zone or the dock; a boundary edit finishes with Done.
- **Zone names match the Lymow app.** Renaming a zone in the Toolkit now writes the name the official app reads, so the two stay in step. A zone you had already renamed in an earlier Toolkit build shows its new name in the app once you rename it one more time.
- **Splitting a zone keeps its settings** — both halves keep the original zone's cutting settings. Merging two zones resets the result to your global settings.

## Cutting settings

- **Saving is quick and confirmed.** A save takes right away, and the Toolkit tells you the mower accepted it — or why it did not — instead of just saying "Saved." This covers global, per-zone, Fleet and scheduled saves.
- **Adaptive Zigzag mows the right pattern.** Picking Adaptive Zigzag now mows that pattern — before it could mow perimeter-laps-only.

## Remote control and camera

- Lower latency, fewer dropped frames and no WiFi/4G flip-flopping while you drive.
- The picture should hand off from WiFi to 4G without a gap when the WiFi signal drops.
- Leaving remote control wakes the mower first, so it can never be left stranded.
- Opening remote control matches the mower's deck height instead of moving it on launch.
- On a fullscreen camera, the **Edit Map** button fades away with the rest of the controls when you are idle and comes back on a touch, and it steps aside while you are driving.

## Fleet Mode

- Every command, setting and camera names the mower it is for, so the wrong mower is never commanded by mistake.
- A mower at another location is skipped by a location-wide command rather than commanded.
- Settings such as the remote deck height are remembered per mower.

## Also

- A reported "warning code 0" means "no fault" and is no longer logged, shown as an alert, or pinned on the map.
- More of the Toolkit is translated into your language.
