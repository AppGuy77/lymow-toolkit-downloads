Lymow Toolkit v2.14.7

The phone's Back button asks before it closes the Toolkit. It keeps your sign-in, settings, maps, layout and history — just update.


- **Back on a phone** — asks "Leave the Toolkit?" instead of closing it.


## New

- **On a phone or tablet, Back asks "Leave the Toolkit?" instead of closing the Toolkit.** Android and iPhone, in the browser and as a Home Screen app. **Stay** keeps the page exactly as it was — nothing reloads, and a popup or the camera that was open stays open. **Leave** goes back to where you came from; if the Toolkit was the first page you opened, press Back once more to close it. In Home Assistant, Home Assistant's own Back applies.
