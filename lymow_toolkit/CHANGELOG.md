An accuracy update for everyone on v2.10.0. It keeps your sign-in, settings, maps, layout and history — just update.


- **Dock & cancel now really cancels the mow** — the "an unfinished mow is stored — it may take itself back out" warning that showed every time you docked is gone once the mow is truly canceled.

## Accurate status, everywhere

The Toolkit now shows only what the mower actually reports — never a stale, guessed, or half-finished value dressed up as current.

- **Dock & cancel truly cancels.** Choosing to cancel when you dock used to leave the mow stored on the mower, so the "an unfinished mow is stored — it may take itself back out to finish it" warning came back after every dock. The Toolkit now ends the mow for real before sending the mower home, and only when a mow was actually running — so the warning clears and stays clear.
- **"Mow on hold" is only a real paused mow.** The on-hold card (and its Resume) no longer appears while the mower is driving to the dock, sitting on the charger, under remote control, or when no mow ever ran. A "0% done" that meant the mower had not mowed anything is gone.
- **Offline and asleep mowers say so.** When a mower is offline, asleep, or idle with no fresh reading, the dashboard marks it **not live** and shows how long ago it last answered; its position is labeled **last known**. In Fleet Mode each mower does the same on its own card.
- **The readings read true.** Battery shows **Charged** at 100% instead of "Charging"; GPS accuracy is shown only when the mower reports it (no more a flat "±0.00 m"); 0 satellites shows as 0, not "?"; and the WiFi/4G tile shows 4G or "—" rather than calling a mower "Connected" when it is on cellular or offline, and never shows the mower's own name as its network.
