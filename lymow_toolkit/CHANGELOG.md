Lymow Toolkit v2.12.1

Zone saves that reach every zone, edits that stay put, and Do Not Disturb as its own switch. It keeps your sign-in, settings, maps, layout and history — just update.


- **Saving to All zones now reaches every zone** and the panel shows the new value right away.
- **A setting you change stays as you set it** until you press Save or reload the page.
- **Do Not Disturb has its own switch** next to Auto Recharge & Resume — each can be turned off on its own.


## Fixed

- **Saving to All zones now reaches every zone.** With several zones selected and a setting showing "Mixed", Save → All zones writes the change into every zone and confirms it with the mower. The panel shows the new value right away; it used to show the zones' old values until the mower sent a new map, which read as the save not having applied.
- **A setting you change stays as you set it** until you press Save or reload the page. The panel refreshes often — a new report from the mower, a new selection — and it no longer paints over the control you are editing; the other controls keep following the mower.

## Changed

- **Do Not Disturb has its own switch.** Settings → Recharge & Resume now has two switches. **Auto Recharge & Resume** is the mower's own switch: it docks to charge at your return level and goes back out at your resume level. **Do Not Disturb** is the hours in which no mow may start — set by you or by the sun and dew. Each can be turned off on its own; with Do Not Disturb off, mows may start at any hour, which the Toolkit writes to the mower the same way the official app does.
