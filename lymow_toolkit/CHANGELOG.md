Lymow Toolkit v2.10.2

A quick game-controller fix for everyone on v2.10.1. It keeps your sign-in, settings, maps, layout and history — just update.


- **Your game controller should reconnect on its own** — turn it off and back on (or step away and come back) and it should drive the mower again right away, with no need to re-run **Set up controller**. Your saved setup is kept.


## Game controller reconnects on its own

When a paired controller was turned off and back on — or the Toolkit page sat in the background for a while — the controller used to go dead until you re-ran **Set up controller**, even though your setup was still saved. The Toolkit now watches for the controller coming back and picks it up on its own, so you just keep driving. Nothing to re-do, and **"Save my settings" does not need to be on** for this — your controller setup saves by itself, per kind of device, the moment you finish it.
