Lymow Toolkit v2.9.1

A fix-up for everyone on v2.9.0. It keeps your sign-in, settings, maps, layout and history — just update.


- **Dashboard text should stay inside its tiles** at every screen size, on phones and computers.
- **Arranging the page:** the ⠿ handles should no longer float over the top bar, and nothing of a drag should stay on screen after a drop.


## The dashboard

- Text should stay inside its tile and inside the dashboard at every screen size and in every language. Long WiFi names,
  positions, fault messages and zone names wrap inside the tile, and a number is never split.
- The tile details (charging, satellites, the 4G line, an unfinished mow) show in your language.

## Arranging the page

- While **🔒 Layout** is unlocked, the ⠿ handles and edge grips should scroll under the top bar instead of floating over it.
- The name that follows the pointer, the drop marker and the faded section should go away as soon as you drop, and also
  if you press Esc, switch windows, or lock or leave the page in the middle of a drag.
